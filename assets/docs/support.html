Getting help

Everything on this page works without an internet connection. If you need to reach us, the online form is at eggboundrush.com/support.html. When you write, it helps to mention your device model and iOS version.

Common questions

An egg disappeared from the board. Logging an egg as collected removes it from the pasture on purpose and adds it to Statistics and History. If it was a mistake, tap Undo in the message that appears right after, or check History to confirm what happened.
I cannot place a decorative object. Some decorative items become available once your Care Points reach a threshold. The Objects screen shows how many points are still needed. Nothing can be bought — points only come from using the app.
An object is outside all of my zones. Zones are geometric areas. Drag the object inside a zone, or open Zones and resize the zone frame so it covers the object.
The app asked for camera access. Only when you tap to add a profile photo. Declining leaves every other feature working exactly as before.
How do I move my data to a new phone? Open Settings → Export data. This produces a JSON file you can save or send to yourself. There is no cloud sync, because the app has no server component.
How do I delete everything? Settings → Reset local data erases the pasture, eggs, zones and history. Deleting the app removes all remaining data from the device.

Troubleshooting

  • If artwork looks blurry, close and reopen the app so it reloads at full resolution.
  • If sounds do not play, check Settings → Sound and your device's silent switch.
  • If the app feels slow with a very full board, group objects into zones and remove items you no longer track.
