How Eggbound Rush works

Eggbound Rush replaces a list or spreadsheet with one visual board. Instead of typing entries, you arrange the pasture the way it actually looks and read its state at a glance.

The board

The main screen is your pasture. Hens, nests, troughs, fences and decorative objects sit where you put them. Drag any object to move it. Tap an object to see which zone it belongs to or to remove it.

Zones

A zone is a named rectangle with a purpose: nesting, feeding, water, free range or collection. Objects belong to a zone based on where they physically sit, so moving an object into a zone is all it takes to reorganise. The Zones screen counts what is inside each area and flags anything left outside.

Tracking eggs

  1. Tap Mark egg and pick a type: white, golden or patterned.
  2. Tap the exact spot on the pasture where you found it.
  3. When you pick the egg up, tap it and choose Log as collected.
  4. The egg leaves the board and appears in Statistics and History.

Tapped something by accident? Use Undo in the message that appears, and the record and its points are rolled back.

Statistics

Every number is calculated on your device from your own records: eggs collected per day, week or month, eggs still waiting on the board, active hens, zones in use and a breakdown by egg type. Nothing is estimated and nothing is fetched from a server.

Care Points

Care Points measure how much you have used the app. Placing an object, marking an egg, logging a collection and creating a zone each add a few points, and the first meaningful action of the day adds a small bonus. Points are never spent and cannot be purchased — they simply unlock extra decorative objects and pasture themes as they accumulate.

Offline by design

There is no account, no sync and no server holding your data. Turn off your connection entirely and every screen keeps working.
