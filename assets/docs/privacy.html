Privacy Policy

Eggbound Rush · Last updated: 24 August 2026

Short version: Eggbound Rush works offline and keeps everything you create on your own device. We do not collect, transmit or sell personal data.

1. Data we collect

None. Eggbound Rush has no account system, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no crash reporting service. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any identifier.

2. Data you create

The pasture layout, zones, egg records, activity history, Care Points, display name and profile photo are stored in the app's private storage area on your device. They are never uploaded. Removing the app removes this data permanently.

3. Camera and photo library

The camera and photo library are used for exactly one purpose: choosing a profile picture. Access is requested only at the moment you tap to add a photo, and you can decline without losing any other functionality. The selected image is cropped on the device and saved to the app's private folder. It is never uploaded, shared or analysed.

4. Network use

All features work with no internet connection. The app may make one short, optional request to our server to refresh the public links shown in Settings and an occasional release note. This request contains no personal data and no identifiers, and the app functions normally if it fails or is blocked.

5. Third parties

We do not share data with third parties, because we do not hold any. The app contains no third-party advertising or tracking components.

6. Children

Eggbound Rush is suitable for all ages. Because no personal data is collected, no data about children is collected either.

7. Your control

  • Export a copy of everything you created, from Settings → Export data.
  • Erase all local data at any time, from Settings → Reset local data.
  • Remove your profile photo at any time, from Profile.
  • Deleting the app deletes all remaining data.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with a new release of the app and be published at eggboundrush.com/privacy-policy.html.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through eggboundrush.com/support.html.
